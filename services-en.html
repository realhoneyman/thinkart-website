Our Curation Services

From the first consultation to the perfect gallery wall — every service is tailored to your space, budget and taste.

A

Private Home Curation

Building an art collection that resonates with your home's architecture, light and character — from living rooms and guest areas to bedrooms and hallways.

B

Office & Business Curation

Designing an art experience in the workplace — reception areas, corridors and meeting rooms that reflect your company's identity.

C

Acquisition & Sourcing

Sourcing rare pieces from galleries, independent artists and auction houses in Israel and worldwide, including authenticity and value checks.

D

Long-Term Collection Building

Ongoing support in growing your collection — with an emphasis on artistic and financial value over time, and continuous guidance for future acquisitions.

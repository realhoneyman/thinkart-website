The Story Behind THINKART

Art curation isn't just choosing pieces — it's listening to a space and the people who live in it.

Who We Are

Personal Art Curation,
Tailored to You

At THINKART, we believe art isn't decoration — it's language. We work personally with every client to understand their space, character and story, and build around them an art collection that feels like it was always there.

Our team combines deep knowledge of the Israeli and international art scene with precise design thinking — the result is spaces that tell a real story. Since founding the studio, we've guided dozens of families and companies in building collections that turn a home or office into a place with identity and story.

1Personal consultation based on your space and personal style
2Exclusive access to artists, galleries and auction houses
3Full support — from selection to professional installation
